I am a first-year PhD student in the AS²ERT Lab at The University of Texas at Dallas (UTD), advised by Prof. Soneya Binta Hossain. My research primarily lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and software engineering (AI4SE). I believe that LLMs are among the most important inventions since writing. This conviction motivates my research, which explores how their potential can be fully translated in software engineering.

Before joining UTD, I earned my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from the University of Palermo and worked as an undergraduate research assistant at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), where I had the opportunity to work with Prof. Darko Marinov.
